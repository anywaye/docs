## What the packet is for

`cafaye/docs/src/content/docs/services/index.md` is the platform's front door — a
table of the services the fleet runs. It listed seven (`identity`, `billing`,
`courier`, `darkroom`, `muse`, `gateway`, `parlor`) and named neither `box`
(the operated control plane) nor `keyholder` (the registry-credential exchange),
and it had no row for `pantry` at all. A docs page that omits the newest
services reads as though the ones it names are the whole fleet.

The source of truth read for the canonical set:

- `moon/PLAN-names-urls-rollout.md` §1 (the locked naming table and the internal
  URL map: `identity`, `billing`, `courier`, `darkroom`, `muse`, `pantry`, `box`
  under `*.svc.cafaye.com`; `gateway` public at `api.cafaye.com`).
- the org repo list, `moon/cafaye/*/` — the fleet is location-locked.
- each service's own `cafaye.yml` for its `description:`, `language:` and
  `dependencies:`. `core` is the source of truth for contracts; these
  repositories are the source of truth for what they are.

I did **not** use the org profile `.github/README.md` as the source of truth: it
is itself stale (it says `darkroom` is Rust and `gateway` is TypeScript, and it
names neither `box` nor `keyholder`). It is another repository's prose and
read-only here; the drift is recorded below, not edited.
