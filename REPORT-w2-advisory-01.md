## Why the docs collection and not a new one

A dated feed is the one shape a hand-written Markdown directory does not
obviously want, and a second content collection was the obvious answer. It is
the wrong one here, and the reason is not effort.

Starlight's sidebar only links pages of the **docs** collection.
`tests/smoke.mjs` walks `src/content/docs/` to assert every page on disk is
reachable from that sidebar. A collection outside that directory is invisible to
both — so every advisory would be a page nothing navigates to and nothing
checks, which is the one failure this repository has the least defence against.
The advisory fields are therefore extended onto the docs schema, kept optional
because the other sixty-odd pages carry no advisory metadata, and
`tests/advisories.mjs` closes the gap optionality opens.

## Every claim was re-derived, not inherited

Both advisories are written for a reader outside cafaye, and every factual claim
in them was read out of the service sources rather than out of this
repository's own prose:

- **~4.4s** — `courier/lib/courier/health.ex`'s moduledoc, with its measurement
  conditions (database taken away under a running pool, release image,
  `postgres:17`). The 2-second query timeout is in the same file
  (`@query_timeout_ms 2_000`), and the moduledoc says the wait is
  `DBConnection` queue backpressure, not the query.
- **`/up` is a boot check** — `kit/templates/kamal/deploy.yml.erb`'s healthcheck
  block, which records that `/up` was a defect, that `identity` and `courier`
  each hand-patched their own copy first, and that the correction was measured
  2026-10-07.
- **the three paths side by side** — `billing/config/routes.rb`, which mounts
  `healthz`, `readyz` and `up` (`rails/health#show`) together. Read directly,
  not from the topology page.
- **who serves what** — `architecture/topology.md`'s per-service table was
  cross-checked against kit's template comment; they agree.

Three corrections came out of that re-derivation and are in the committed pages:

1. advisory 002 said `billing` serves two probes. It serves **three** —
   `/healthz`, `/readyz` and Rails' `/up`, mounted side by side.
2. advisory 002 said "every adopter had hand-patched their own copy". The
   sources support **`identity` and `courier`**, which is what it now says.
3. the index claimed "cafaye publishes no package a third party consumes" — a
   fleet-wide negative this repository cannot re-derive offline. Dropped; the
   page now says what an advisory is instead.

Nothing was invented. Both conditions are ones the fleet observed and recorded;
neither is a hypothetical. The `source:` field is required and the test refuses
one that is not path-shaped, because a claim that cannot be re-derived is a
claim a reader cannot check.

## Known limitations, said plainly

- **The status line for advisory 001 is `open` and stays `open`.** The fix is
  the operator's probe timeout, not a change to courier. Nothing in courier was
  edited, and nothing in this repository pretends otherwise.
- **`tests/advisories.mjs` cannot verify the source file exists.** That needs a
  fleet checkout, which the offline tier must not require. It asserts the source
  is named and path-shaped; verifying it against the fleet is a contract-tier
  concern and is not built.
- **Two advisories, not ten.** The library table in `CAFAYE-OPS.md` has ten
  rows. The brief asked for two. The other eight are not written because writing
  them was not asked for, and a half-verified advisory is worse than none.
- **This worktree had two writers in it.** A recovery daemon snapshotted
  uncommitted work mid-session (commits `8bbe061`, `fe359fe`). Everything it
  committed was verified by two full gate runs before being kept; nothing it
  touched was weakened to get there. The stray `brief.txt` it re-committed is
  removed again, per `310e141`.

## What a successor should do first

1. Read `tests/advisories.mjs` before adding a third advisory — it derives the
   expected index from the pages, so a new advisory fails until the index lists
   it. That is the intended direction.
2. The next two advisories worth writing from the library table are the
   `identity` MFA routes (404 from an unset `MFA_ENCRYPTION_KEY`, absent rather
   than broken, no error ever printed) and `gateway`'s per-process sessions

   with a measurement behind them, not suspicions.

   intended direction; lowering it is a deletion of a check.
4. If the 29 `github.com/cafaye/<repo>` 404s are ever going to be fixed, the fix
   is the repositories becoming public, not this site.
